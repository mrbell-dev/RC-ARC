# POTA Activation 7/15/26 at the Blue Ridge Parkway Overlook – Photos
On Wednesday July 15, 2026, *Rowan ARS* members Bob T. and Rick N., along with fellow Amateur friends of theirs, enjoyed a great POTA outing at the Blue Ridge Parkway overlook. Rick shared some of the photos from the event.

Most notably, Bob was able to use and learn his new-to-him ICOM IC-7300 rig that he purchased at the *Firecracker Hamfest* the previous weekend. Bob had recently earned his General license. From what we hear, Bob's been really taking of his new HF capabilities!
