## RARS 2026 Field Day
**The 2026 Rowan ARS annual ARRL Field Day was a great *success*. Members are already talking about 2027**

[*Ham it up: Rowan radio society joins nat’l event*](https://www.salisburypost.com/2026/06/30/ham-it-up-rowan-radio-society-joins-natl-event/) Salisbury Post article by Janet Briggs

## When & Where

- **Dates:** Saturday, June 27 -- Sunday, June 28, 2026
- **On-Air Operating Begins:** 2:00 PM Saturday (runs around the clock into Sunday)
- **Location:** Rowan County Rescue Squad, 1140 Julian Rd, Salisbury, NC 28146


## Stations

This year's Field Day will feature a range of stations and activities, including:

- **HF Voice and CW Stations** -- Reach across the country and log contacts on the HF bands. Mark K logged a good number of CW (Morse Code) contacts.
- **Digital Modes** -- See how modern software turns weak signals into reliable communication. We had two FT8 stations and made a number of contacts!
- **Portable and Emergency Setups** -- Watch a team deploy antennas, power sources, and radios in real-world conditions.
- **Get On The Air** -- New to the hobby? Newcomers and Technician licensees were welcomed to make contacts on the HF bands under the supervision of a licensed control operator, all under a club call sign **W4EXU** at our GOTA station.

## Antennas

We featured a number of antennas including YAGI-Uda directional, dipole, End-Fed Half-wave, and a vertical antenna. We ate well, thanks to the Secretary, Doug!
